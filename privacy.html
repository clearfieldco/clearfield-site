Privacy notice

Last updated November 25, 2026

Clearfield & Co. ("Clearfield," "we") prepares comps for small business owners. This notice explains what we collect when you use clearfieldcomps.com and request comps, and how we use it.

What we collect

  • What you enter in our request form: your name, email, website, what you sell, where you sell most, your monthly revenue range and, if you choose, your package.
  • What you tell us during onboarding: your answers to our onboarding questionnaire after you order.
  • What you enter in our demo form: your first name and email.
  • Payment details: payments are handled by Stripe on Stripe's own pages. We receive confirmation of payment, your email and the business details you give at checkout. We never see or store your full card number.
  • Your P&L, if you send it: only for the Comps + Your Numbers package.
  • Emails you send us.

We do not use advertising or tracking cookies on this site. Numbers you type into the Clearfield Comps demo stay in your browser and are never sent to us.

How we use it

  • To find comps that match your business and to review your website and strategy.
  • To contact you about your request, take payment and deliver your comps.
  • To email you the link to the Clearfield Comps demo and, after that, occasional notes about Clearfield. Every one of those emails has an unsubscribe link.
  • To issue refunds under our money-back guarantee.

We do not sell your information. We do not share your name, your business or your P&L with other customers. Your own numbers are never used as a comp for anyone else without your explicit permission.

Who processes it for us

We use a small number of service providers to run the business: Netlify (website hosting and form submissions), Kit (email delivery for demo links and our newsletter), Stripe (payments, receipts and refunds) and Google Workspace (email). They process information only to provide those services.

How long we keep it

We keep request and order records for as long as needed to serve you and to meet tax and accounting requirements. You can ask us to delete your information at any time, subject to those requirements.

Your choices

You can ask to see, correct or delete the information we hold about you by replying to any email from us or by sending a request through the request form.

Changes

If we change this notice, we will update the date at the top of this page.
