Each P&L standardized to the same lines. Net revenue is sales minus refunds and chargebacks. Neither P&L records owner pay, so profit is before the owner is paid.

What stands out

01

Product cost is the difference.

Comp B keeps 75 cents of every dollar after product and shipping. Comp A keeps 45. That 30-point gap is nearly the size of Comp A's entire ad budget.

02

Comp A gets more from its ads and still earns less.

Comp A brings in $2.91 of sales per $1 of ads. Comp B brings in $1.92. What decides how much you can spend on ads is what you keep after product cost, not how efficient the ads look.

03

Comp B depends on a single ad platform.

Over half of Comp B's revenue goes to ads on one platform. A policy change or account problem there would hit most of its sales at once.

Questions for your business

  • What do you keep after product and shipping? Above 70% like Comp B, or under 50% like Comp A?
  • Your breakeven return on ads is 1 divided by your gross margin. At 45% that's $2.22 of sales per $1. Do you know yours?
  • How many ad platforms do your sales depend on?
