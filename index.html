Questions

What are Clearfield Comps?

Comps are the real profit and loss statements (P&Ls) of two or three businesses like yours, matched on niche, business model and monthly revenue. We line them up side by side, with every line shown as a share of revenue, and review your website and strategy against them.

What do you need from me?

Only your website. No financials, no calls. If you choose Comps + Your Numbers, you also send us your P&L so we can place it next to your comps.

Where do the comps come from?

From the actual financials of businesses that have sold or are listed for sale, sourced from licensed data and partners who share with permission. Every comp is anonymized: no names, locations or dates, and revenue is shown as a range.

Who is it for?

Owners, operators and general managers of businesses doing $10K to $100K a month who want to grow. It's built for owners who are building, not for owners who are actively selling.
