Questions

Comps are the real profit and loss statements (P&Ls) of two to four businesses like yours, matched on niche, business model and monthly revenue. We line them up side by side, with every line shown as a share of revenue, and review your website and strategy against them.

Only your website. For Comps + Analyst, you also send your P&L before the call.

Where do the comps come from?

From the actual financials of businesses that have sold or are listed for sale, sourced from licensed data and partners who share with permission. Every comp is anonymized: no names, locations or dates, and revenue is shown as a range.
