Questions

What are Clearfield Comps?

Comps are the real profit and loss statements (P&Ls) of businesses like yours, matched on niche, business model and monthly revenue. We line them up side by side, with every line shown in dollars and as a share of sales, and review your website and strategy against them.

What do you need from me?

To start, your website, what you sell, where you sell it and your monthly revenue range. After you order, a short onboarding questionnaire fills in the rest. For Comps + Analyst, you also send your P&L before the call.

Will I see the names of my comps?

No. The names, websites and other identifying details of every comp stay confidential. That’s the industry standard, and our agreements with the businesses and data partners behind each comp require it. You get the full books, matched on niche, model and size, and every comp carries the Clearfield Guarantee: accurate, and drawn from a real business that has sold or been listed for sale.

Who works on my case?

Your own analyst, vetted and experienced in the industry, from first match to final notes. Your comps come from licensed data and private partners that aren’t open to the public, and picking the right ones takes judgment. That’s the job.

What kinds of businesses do you cover?

Most often online stores, Amazon brands, content and affiliate sites, software and SaaS, and agencies and online services. We also cover businesses like YouTube channels, newsletters, mobile apps, online courses and memberships, subscription boxes and TikTok Shop sellers, but we’re not limited to these. Tell us your website and we’ll confirm we have good comps for you before you pay.

I make under $10K a month, or over $100K. Can this work for me?

Yes. Most of our customers do $10K to $100K a month, but we match comps for businesses above and below that range. Tell us your numbers and we'll find your peers.
